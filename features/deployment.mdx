InkLoom deploys your documentation to a global edge network in a single step. Whether you click a button in the dashboard or run a CLI command in CI, your pages are live at the edge in roughly two seconds — with automatic SSL and instant cache invalidation included at every tier.

## One-click deploy

The fastest way to deploy is directly from the project dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your project">
    Navigate to your project in the InkLoom dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Deploy">
    Click the **Deploy** button in the top-right corner of the project overview. InkLoom builds your pages, optimizes assets, and pushes to the edge automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your live URL">
    Once the deployment completes, your live documentation URL is shown in the deployment confirmation panel. Each deployment also receives a unique ID you can reference later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deploy from the CLI

For CI pipelines or scripted workflows, use the `inkloom deploy` command:

```bash
inkloom deploy proj_abc --production
# ✓ Building pages (24 pages)  0.8s
# ✓ Optimizing assets          0.3s
# ✓ Deploying to edge          1.1s
# ✓ Deployment complete
# URL: https://docs.acme.com
# ID: dep_7f3k9x · 2.2s total
```

Replace `proj_abc` with your project ID, found in **Dashboard > Settings > General**. The `--production` flag promotes the deployment to your primary domain. Omit it to create a preview deployment instead.

## Edge network

Every InkLoom deployment runs on Cloudflare Pages, distributed across 300+ edge locations worldwide. This means your readers load documentation from a server that's geographically close to them — typically under 50ms response times globally.

Key network features:

- **Automatic SSL** — TLS certificates are provisioned and renewed automatically for every domain, including custom domains.
- **Instant cache invalidation** — when you deploy, the previous version is purged from every edge node simultaneously. Readers always see the latest content within seconds.
- **DDoS protection** — Cloudflare's network absorbs traffic spikes without any configuration on your part.

## Custom domains

<Note>
  Custom domains require the **Pro plan** or above. On the Free plan, your docs are served from `<project>.inkloom.dev`.
</Note>

To serve your docs from your own domain (for example, `docs.acme.com`):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open domain settings">
    Go to **Dashboard > project > Settings > Domains** and click **Add Domain**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your domain">
    Type your desired domain or subdomain (for example, `docs.acme.com`) and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a CNAME record">
    In your DNS provider, create a CNAME record pointing your domain to `inkloom.dev`:

    | Type | Name | Value |
    |------|------|-------|
    | CNAME | `docs` | `inkloom.dev` |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for propagation">
    DNS changes typically propagate within a few minutes. InkLoom automatically detects the record and provisions your SSL certificate. The domain status in Settings will change to **Active** once everything is verified.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Deployment history

Every deployment is recorded in your project's deployment log, accessible from **Dashboard > project > Deployments**. Each entry shows:

- **Deployment ID** — a unique identifier like `dep_7f3k9x` that you can use to reference or roll back to a specific build.
- **Timestamp** — the exact date and time the deployment was triggered.
- **Status** — `Success`, `Failed`, or `In progress`.
- **Source** — whether the deployment was triggered from the dashboard, CLI, or a GitHub Action.

To roll back to a previous deployment, click the deployment entry and select **Promote to Production**. The rollback deploys in the same time as a normal deployment.
