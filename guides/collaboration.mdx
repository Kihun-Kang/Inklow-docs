InkLoom is built for documentation teams, not just solo writers. You can invite colleagues with the right level of access, discuss changes through inline comments without leaving the editor, and — on the Ultimate plan — see your teammates' cursors moving in real time as everyone works on the same page at once. Whether your team is two people or two hundred, InkLoom's collaboration tools keep everyone aligned.

## Inviting team members

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Members settings">
    In the [InkLoom dashboard](https://app.inkloom.dev), select your project and go to **Settings > Members**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite Member**. Enter the email address of the person you want to invite, then use the **Role** dropdown to select the appropriate role for them (see the **Roles** section below for details).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and send">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The invitee receives an email with a link to accept the invitation and join your InkLoom project. If they do not already have an InkLoom account, they will be prompted to create one first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can resend or revoke a pending invitation at any time from the **Settings > Members** page by clicking the **…** menu next to the invitee's email address.
</Tip>

## Roles

InkLoom uses role-based access control to ensure team members can only do what they need to. Assign roles carefully — you can always change a member's role later from **Settings > Members**.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Owner">
    Owners have unrestricted access to the project. An Owner can:

    - Create, edit, publish, and delete any page
    - Manage billing and upgrade or downgrade the plan
    - Invite and remove members, and change any member's role
    - Delete the project permanently

    Each project must have at least one Owner at all times.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Editor">
    Editors are the core contributors to your documentation. An Editor can:

    - Create, edit, and publish pages
    - Upload images and other assets
    - Leave and resolve inline comments
    - View project settings (but not change billing or delete the project)

    Assign the Editor role to writers, developers, and anyone who contributes content regularly.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Viewer">
    Viewers have read-only access to your documentation project inside the InkLoom dashboard. A Viewer can:

    - Read all published and draft pages
    - Leave inline comments on pages

    Assign the Viewer role to stakeholders, reviewers, or external contractors who need to read and comment but should not make edits.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Inline comments

Inline comments let you and your team discuss specific parts of a page without switching to a separate tool. Comments are anchored to the exact text they refer to, so context is never lost.

To leave a comment on a page:

1. Open the page in the InkLoom editor.
2. **Select the text** you want to comment on by clicking and dragging.
3. A small **comment bubble** icon (💬) appears at the edge of the selection — click it.
4. Type your comment in the input that appears and press **Submit** (or hit `Cmd+Enter` / `Ctrl+Enter`).

Your comment appears in the right-hand comment panel and is anchored to the highlighted text. Teammates with Editor or Viewer access can reply directly in the thread.

Once a discussion is resolved, click **Resolve** on the comment thread. Resolved comments are hidden from the default view but remain accessible in the **Resolved** tab of the comment panel, so you always have a full audit trail.

<Tip>
  Use comments to flag sections that need a technical review, request specific edits, or leave context for future writers — they are a much cleaner alternative to "TODO" comments left in the page content itself.
</Tip>

## Real-time editing

On the Ultimate plan, multiple team members can edit the same page simultaneously. Each active editor's cursor appears in a distinct color with their name attached, so you can see exactly where your colleagues are working and avoid stepping on each other's changes.

Real-time editing is automatic — there is nothing to enable. As long as two or more Editors open the same page at the same time, InkLoom enters collaborative mode and syncs keystrokes across all sessions in real time. Changes are merged using operational transformation, so conflicting edits are handled gracefully without data loss.

<Note>
  Real-time editing with live cursors requires the **Ultimate plan**. On the Pro plan, multiple editors can work on the same project, but not on the same page simultaneously — the last save wins. Upgrade to Ultimate in **Settings > Billing** to unlock real-time collaboration.
</Note>
